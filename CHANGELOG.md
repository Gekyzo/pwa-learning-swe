## 0.6.4 - 2026-11-25

- Card rich text (CARD-10): a card `body` is a Markdown subset: a blank line starts a paragraph, one `\n` is a line break, `**text**` is bold and `*text*` is italic; a stray `*` stays text. New pure module `src/richtext.js` (`parseRich`, `plainText`, `richStats`, `bodyIssues`, `MIN_PARAGRAPHS` 2, `MAX_PARAGRAPHS` 4, `MAX_BOLD` 3, `MAX_BOLD_LENGTH` 60). The body limit of CARD-2 counts the visible text: `MAX_BODY` 600, and `MAX_BODY_SOURCE` 800 for the raw body with its markup. `bodyNode` of `src/ui/card.js` builds `<p>`, `<strong>`, `<em>` and `<br>` nodes; part `feed-card-strong`. New script `npm run check:bodies [pack...]` prints the style issues of each body. The 132 cards of `content/` are rewritten in English and Spanish to 2 to 4 paragraphs with bold and italic; each `rev` is unchanged: the facts are the same, so no review restarts.
- Deploy gate: the content test `each body follows the style rules, in each language (CARD-10)` applies `bodyIssues` to each body and translation. `.github/workflows/pages.yml` has a `test` job (`npm test` on Node 22), and `build` needs it, so a release tag with a failed test deploys nothing (VER-9).

## 0.6.3 - 2026-11-25

- Blank screen after an update for a signed-in user (SW-4, SW-6): `respond` of `sw.js` matched with `ignoreSearch`, and the oldest entry won, the page stored under the sign-in return `./?code=…&state=…`, which `refresh` never replaced. That page of an old deploy had no `#diagram` in its import map, so the modules of 0.6.1 did not resolve. New `cacheKey(url)` drops the query for each `put` and `match`; the cache is `pwa-learning-swe-shell-v2`, so activation deletes the v1 entries.

## 0.6.1 - 2026-11-25

- Card diagrams (CARD-9): a card may carry `diagram: { svg, alt }`, and a translation its `diagram.alt`. New pure module `src/diagram.js` (`parseDiagram(svg, id)`, `CLASSES`, `MAX_DIAGRAM` 6000): a strict parser of an SVG subset into a tree, with allowlists of elements, attributes per element, `d-` classes and plain values, ids and marker references prefixed by the card id, and the XML entities only. `normalizeCard` keeps a valid diagram and drops a bad one (`MAX_ALT` 400); `cardTexts` needs the alt of a translation. `diagramSvg` of `src/ui/controls.js` builds the nodes from the tree, with `aria-label`; `cardNode` puts it after the title in part `feed-card-diagram`. New component `diagram` in `styles.css` (the `d-` classes, token `--diagram-max`). `docs/prompts/` holds the prompt for the AI, embeddings and RAG packs and a sample card.
- Three packs with 90 cards (CARD-6): `content/llm.json` (30 cards, 10 diagrams), `content/embeddings.json` (30 cards, 11 diagrams) and `content/rag.json` (30 cards, 11 diagrams), each with Spanish text, in `content/index.json` and in `SHELL` of `sw.js`. `rag-pipeline` is the sample card of `docs/prompts/`, unchanged.
