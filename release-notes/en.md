# Release notes

What changes for you in each version of the app. The newest version is first.

## Unreleased

- Beside the feed (or after the first cards on a phone) you see how many reviews you did today, the cards that come back in the next 7 days, how your seen cards are doing and the counts of each topic.
- Under the feed title, a greeting says how many cards come back today and how many new ones wait for you.
- Each card of the feed says why it is there: **Review** for a card that comes back, **New** for a card you have not seen.
- The app has a new look: pure black in the dark theme, with the cards as the only surface.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-10-31

- **What's new** shows bold text in bold, no longer between asterisks.
- The introduction now shows the whole feed in four pages: the cards and **Like**, the review and its grades, **Save** and **More**, and how the app works offline.
- Choose your topics on the last page of the introduction, and change them in **Settings › Topics**. The feed shows only the cards of the topics that are on; Saved keeps every saved card.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-31

- The feed is here: scroll through 42 cards on Git, HTTP, databases, JavaScript, testing and software design. Each card is a concept, a piece of code, a pitfall, a quiz or a resource to read. The cards are in English.
- A card you saw comes back for review a day later, then after longer and longer waits while you remember it. A quiz answer, **Remembered**, **Forgot** and **I knew this** set the next review.
- **Like** asks for more cards of a topic. **More** hides a card that is too hard, not of interest, or wrong.
- **Saved** keeps the cards you save. You can turn it off in **Settings › Modules**.
- The notes are gone. A backup of notes cannot be imported.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-10-30

- First version of LearnSWE, with its own icon. It works offline, installs on Android and has light and dark themes in English and Spanish. Sign in with Google, or use it with no account. **Delete all data** in Settings starts the app as new. The feed of software content comes next.
